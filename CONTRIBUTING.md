# Contributing

Contributions that improve clarity, safety, testing, portability, and professional usefulness are welcome.

## Before opening a Pull Request

- Keep each change focused on one plugin or one shared governance concern.
- Use synthetic or fully anonymized test materials.
- Do not include client data, secrets, private templates, local paths, caches, logs, or generated work products.
- Preserve third-party copyright, license, and attribution requirements.
- Document the intended use, non-use cases, dependencies, and lawyer-review boundary.
- Add or update tests for material behavior changes.

## Legal and professional boundaries

Contributions must not present generated output as a substitute for qualified legal review. Jurisdiction-specific rules must identify their scope and source requirements.

By submitting a contribution, you represent that you have the right to provide it under the license applicable to the relevant plugin or Skill.
